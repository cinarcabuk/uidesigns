The round 2 hairline E with the new button layout. Everything stays hairline: one-pixel rings for flash and the custom button, and a thin frame around the camera roll.

Left
Shutter. Its ring is the largest element on the deck.
Top middle
Flash, which replaces the star from your sketch. Tap to cycle Off, On and Auto. Auto fires only when the scene is dark. When flash is on, a small ON flag appears in the top margin.
Middle
The custom button. It flips the camera by default. Hold it to switch it to a self-timer, the grid or double exposure. The dot on its rim marks it as customizable.
Right
The camera roll, as the tall rectangle from your sketch. It shows your last frame and how many frames are left.

Silver

The same layout set in brushed aluminum, like a chrome camera body. The image sits in a recessed black window, so it still has black around it on every side. Type is engraved dark. The meter needle is red, and the shutter is chrome.

  • The brushed surface, the chrome shutter, the black buttons and the phone frame all carry a highlight that moves with tilt.
  • Flash and the custom button are glossy black. Their highlight moves too.
  • The editor uses the same metal, with the photo set into a black recess.
Reflection follows your pointer

About the gyro: yes, this works in the real app. iOS Core Motion and Android's rotation sensor give the phone's attitude. That angle moves the highlight band, the same way it moves here. This page tries the browser's motion sensor first, but the claude.ai viewer blocks motion sensors, so here your pointer stands in for the tilt. On a phone, open index.html from the repo in Safari or Chrome over https to feel the real gyro.

Around the frame

Mode, ƒ, film and EV sit in the top margin. Shutter speed, the small meter and ISO sit in the bottom margin. Tap a readout and its dial replaces the lens selector for a few seconds.

The meter

The needle runs on a spring with real overshoot. Spot-meter by tapping the image: a dark area makes the needle swing. Firing the shutter kicks the needle as well.

Editing, four sections

Exp. · Film · Texture · Print, with the histogram top right and undo / redo bottom right, as in your sketch.
