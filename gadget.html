E, as an old gadget from the future.

Back to E's world, pushed toward a device that looks old and futuristic at once. Each one is very simple from arm's length and has tiny details up close. The layout is E's: small readouts around a black-margined frame, a compact meter, the lens row, and shutter, flash, custom button and camera roll. Each film now carries its own two- or three-color signature, and every screen shows it somewhere. Texture comes in a later pass.
