Ten styles, from scratch

A clean break from the black and silver rounds. Each screen takes its look from a different object or era: lab equipment, blueprints, wood and brass, toys, terminals, signs, 80s compacts, glass and tape. Every one still has the parts a film camera needs: a viewfinder, settings, a meter, film, lens, flash, shutter and camera roll. These are still screens to compare.

Round 5. The earlier pages are unchanged: round 3 (E in black and silver) and round 4 (ten approaches). Mixing is welcome, for example the Cassette's reel counter on the Waist-Level camera.
